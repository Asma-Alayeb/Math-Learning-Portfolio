01 / Algebra · Interactive graph · 5 minutes

Function Quest

Match a target parabola by shifting and reflecting your curve. Three missions connect visual movement with algebraic notation.

Learning goal: Interpret horizontal shifts, vertical shifts, and reflections, including the reversed sign inside parentheses.

Play Function Quest
Inside the learning design

The sequence begins with a visible vertex, moves to equation interpretation, and combines shifts with reflection. Feedback identifies the feature that needs revision, with a more explicit hint after another attempt.

02 / Algebra · Error analysis

Equation Detective

Find the first step that changes the solution. A correct answer matters; knowing why it is correct matters more.

Case file
3(x + 2) = 15
Step 1: 3x + 2 = 15
Step 2: 3x = 13
Step 3: x = 13/3

Where does the reasoning first go wrong?

Choose a step to investigate.

Learning goal: Preserve equivalent statements and distinguish an original error from its consequences.

Inside the learning design

This task makes a common misconception visible. Feedback directs attention to distributing across both terms, then gives a repaired solution learners can verify.

03 / Statistics · Probability exploration

Two Tosses, One Pattern

Is one head more likely than two heads? Change the probability of heads and inspect all three outcomes.

Learning goal: Connect independent outcomes to a discrete probability distribution.

Inside the learning design

For two independent tosses with the same probability p, the probabilities are (1 − p)², 2p(1 − p), and p². The middle outcome includes two sequences: HT and TH.

Teaching approach

Engagement with a mathematical purpose.

01

Start with a question

Give learners something they can predict, investigate, or resolve before introducing a formal rule.

02

Make thinking visible

Use visual representations, meaningful interactions, and well-sequenced practice to connect actions with ideas.

03

Use feedback to move forward

Address the misconception, preserve room for another attempt, and help learners explain their reasoning.

About Asma

Mathematical depth.

My course development includes lessons, assessments, and feedback in Canvas, D2L, and MyOpenMath. As course captain for College Trigonometry, I curated and contributed to a shared OER course shell.

I bring training in effective college instruction, online teaching, Universal Design, and front-end development to the design of clear, accessible learning experiences.
